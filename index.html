Gross Development Value Calculator

A GDV calculator estimates gross development value as net internal area multiplied by the achievable resale price per sqft. The residual workbench below then subtracts build, fees, s106, CIL, BNG, SDLT, contingency, finance and your target profit to give a max land bid. The default is nine houses in Oldham: build cost is the North West BCIS estate housing median as reprinted in Oldham Council's December 2025 viability study, and the sales value is the £4,384 per sqm (about £407 per sqft) that study adopts for a two-bed house in its higher value zone. The regional BCIS table has build costs and new-build sales values for your region. Sensitivity heatmap stresses GDV down 15 percent and build cost up 20 percent. →BCIS via Oldham study· Dec 2025

Worked example, in one paragraph

Three new-build terrace houses in Leeds LS6: 2,508 sqft saleable x £343/sqft gives a GDV of £860,244. At 65 percent LTGDV senior debt sizes to £559,159. Build at the Yorkshire BCIS estate housing median of £1,441/sqm, add fees, s106, contingency and finance, and the max land bid at 20 percent profit on cost is £239,017. Then stress GDV down 10 percent in the heatmap. See the Leeds LS6 walkthrough for the full ledger.

Why this workbench

  • BCIS build cost for the 9 English regions and Wales, each from a named council viability study. Editable.
  • 5 x 5 sensitivity matrix on the right rail, not three pages deep.
  • s106, CIL and BNG modelled per unit, not a black-box percentage.
  • No signup. No paywall. No affiliate links on the output.
  • Profit on cost and profit on GDV shown side by side.

What it does not do

  • Replace a RICS Red Book valuation. Indicative figures only.
  • Auto-fetch live comparables. GDV per sqft is yours to enter.
  • Run a daylight or BRE 209 sunlight study. That kills schemes pre-residual.
  • Underwrite your senior facility. Lenders run their own monitor.
