Residual Method vs Comparable Method

Residual method back-solves land value from GDV minus costs minus profit. Comparable method values land by reference to recent transactions for similar sites. RICS Red Book prefers comparable where evidence exists; falls back to residual when sites are atypical. PPG-Viability often requires both.

Comparable method

  • Reference recent transactions for similar sites in similar locations.
  • Adjust for date, size, density, planning consent, infrastructure.
  • Robust where evidence exists; falls over on bespoke or first-mover sites.

Residual method

  • Back-solve: GDV minus costs minus profit = land value.
  • Strong for atypical sites where comparable evidence is thin.
  • Weakness: highly sensitive to GDV and build-cost assumptions; small input changes shift the answer by 20 to 30 percent.

When PPG-Viability requires both

PPG-Viability Dec 2024 typically expects both. Comparable as a sense-check anchor; residual as the lead method on viability-marginal sites. Where the two diverge by more than 20 percent the appraisal narrative must explain why.

Lender preference

Lenders typically run their own residual in monitor-survey form and reference comparable as a market-evidence test. Treat the two as complements, not alternatives.
