What this workbench has that others do not

  • Five-by-five sensitivity heatmap on the hero, not paywalled.
  • Section 106, CIL and BNG modelled as per-unit cost lines, not a percentage.
  • Both PoC and PoGDV shown side by side.
  • BCIS Q2-2026 regional rates load by default, fully editable.
  • Indicative SDLT-on-land gross-up baked in.
  • No signup. No affiliate links on the calculator output.

What this workbench does not have

  • No live Land Registry comparables auto-fetch (you enter GDV).
  • No daylight or BRE 209 study (specialist tool needed).
  • No PDF export with brand template (planned for v2).
  • No multi-phase scheme modelling (single-phase only).

Methodology transparency

We cite primary sources on every page (BCIS, NPPF, PPG-Viability, Land Registry, HMRC, Revenue Scotland, WRA, UK Finance, Bank of England). Vendor sites that mark up these same sources without attribution do not pass the RICS Red Book sourcing test.
