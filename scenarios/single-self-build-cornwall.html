VAT mechanics

HMRC VAT431NB DIY housebuilders reclaim recovers 20 percent VAT on materials. Labour from a VAT-registered builder is zero-rated; do not reclaim. Submit via the HMRC online portal within 6 months of completion. Indicative recovery on a 180 sqm self-build at mid spec: £25,000 to £35,000.

Cornwall caveat

Cornwall's second-home council tax premium (200 percent from 1 Apr 2025) hits resale velocity in PL postcodes. Apply a 3 to 6 month sales-period extension if the build is for sale rather than owner-occupation; finance interest roll-up rises accordingly.
