Single Self-Build, Cornwall PL

One 180 sqm three-bed family house, Cornwall PL postcode, mid spec. Build cost £1,559/sqm. At a 15 percent profit-on-cost target the scheme supports a maximum land bid of £210,174 on a GDV of £625,974.

Inputs

  • Units: 1. GIA 1,938 sqft (180.0 sqm); NIA 95% (1,841 sqft saleable).
  • GDV: £340/sqft of NIA. GDV per sqft is this scenario's assumption; check it against Land Registry sold prices for detached houses near the plot.
  • Build cost: £1,559/sqm GIA, BCIS estate housing median, South West England, as reprinted in the Cotswold District Local Plan Viability Assessment by Quintic Advisory for Cotswold District Council (May 2026; BCIS data last updated 16 May 2026, rebased to Cotswold (location index 101)). That figure is rebased to Cotswold, not Cornwall, and is for estate housing; a one-off house priced from your own drawings will differ. BCIS figures exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT.
  • Fees 8%, contingency 10%, finance 9.75% over 14 months, target profit 15% on cost: working assumptions, not quoted rates.

Senior debt check

At 65% loan to GDV the senior facility sizes to £406,883. Against total cost including the land bid and SDLT of £573,110, that is 71.0% loan to cost, so check which of your lender's two caps binds first (LTGDV vs LTC).

VAT mechanics

A self-builder can reclaim VAT paid on eligible building materials through the HMRC DIY housebuilders scheme (VAT431NB), claimed after completion. A VAT-registered builder constructing a new dwelling should zero-rate their own services, so there is no VAT on that labour to reclaim. The reclaim is not deducted in the ledger above, because the amount depends on your materials spend.

Cornwall caveat

Cornwall charges a council tax premium on second homes. If the house is built for sale rather than to live in, allow for a longer sales period, which raises the finance cost in the workbench.
