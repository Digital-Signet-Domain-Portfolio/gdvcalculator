Office to 12 Flats, Croydon CR0

Vacant 1980s office building, Croydon CR0, converted to 12 flats at nationally described space standard minimums under Class MA prior approval. Build cost £1,750/sqm. At a 22 percent profit-on-cost target the scheme supports a maximum land bid of £1,097,308 on a GDV of £2,981,664.

Inputs

  • Units: 12. GIA 6,960 sqft (646.6 sqm); NIA 84% (5,846 sqft saleable).
  • GDV: £510/sqft of NIA. GDV per sqft is this scenario's assumption; check it against Land Registry sold prices for converted flats in CR0.
  • Build cost: £1,750/sqm GIA, conversion cost is this scenario's assumption, not a BCIS figure: no public viability study this site draws on reprints BCIS conversion rates. Replace it with a quantity surveyor's figure for the actual building. BCIS figures exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT.
  • Fees 11%, contingency 12.5%, finance 10.5% over 14 months, target profit 22% on cost: working assumptions, not quoted rates.

Senior debt check

At 65% loan to GDV the senior facility sizes to £1,938,082. Against total cost including the land bid and SDLT of £2,651,756, that is 73.1% loan to cost, so check which of your lender's two caps binds first (LTGDV vs LTC).

Every total above is computed by the same engine as the appraisal workbench from the inputs listed. Change any input there to rerun the scheme on your own figures, and use the regional BCIS figures for the build cost.

Caveats

  • Prior approval covers transport, contamination, flooding, noise, natural light and fire safety among other matters; a scheme whose flats cannot reach adequate natural light is refused before the residual matters.
  • Class MA requires the building to have been vacant for a continuous period of at least 3 months before the application.
  • Prior-approval studies, asbestos, structural and M&E surveys are not itemised here: budget them inside the fees or contingency.
