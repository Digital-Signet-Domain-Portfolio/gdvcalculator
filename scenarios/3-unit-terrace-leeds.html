3-Unit Terrace, Leeds LS6

Three two-bed mid-terrace houses, LS6 (Hyde Park, Leeds). Build cost £1,441/sqm. At a 20 percent profit-on-cost target the scheme supports a maximum land bid of £239,017 on a GDV of £860,244.

Inputs

  • Units: 3. GIA 2,850 sqft (264.8 sqm); NIA 88% (2,508 sqft saleable).
  • GDV: £343/sqft of NIA. GDV per sqft is this scenario's assumption; check it against Land Registry sold prices for new-build terraces in LS6.
  • Build cost: £1,441/sqm GIA, BCIS estate housing median, Yorkshire and the Humber, as reprinted in the City of York CIL Viability Study Addendum (CSD5) by Porter Planning Economics for City of York Council (June 2025; BCIS rebased to 1Q 2025 and York (location index 97)). BCIS figures exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT.
  • Fees 11%, contingency 7.5%, finance 9.5% over 14 months, target profit 20% on cost: working assumptions, not quoted rates.
