Mixed-Use Shop + 3 Flats, Brighton

Ground-floor shop with three two-bed flats above, Brighton North Laine, 320 sqm GIA in total. Build cost £1,886/sqm. At a 22 percent profit-on-cost target the scheme supports a maximum land bid of £466,669 on a GDV of £1,483,231.

Inputs

  • GIA 3,444 sqft (320.0 sqm); NIA 85% (2,927 sqft saleable).
  • GDV: Shop: net rent £28,160 (£32,000 less 12%) / 6.5% yield; Flats: 3 x £350,000. The shop rent (£32,000 a year), the 12 percent non-recoverable cost allowance, the 6.5 percent yield and the flat price are this scenario's assumptions.
  • Build cost: £1,886/sqm GIA, BCIS flats median, South East England, as reprinted in the Local Plan Viability Assessment 2025 Update by HDH Planning for Medway Council (June 2025; BCIS data last updated 31 May 2025, rebased to Kent (location index 109)), applied to the whole building, shop floor included. BCIS figures exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT.
  • Fees 12%, contingency 10%, finance 10% over 16 months, target profit 22% on cost: working assumptions, not quoted rates.

Senior debt check

At 65% loan to GDV the senior facility sizes to £964,100. Against total cost including the land bid and SDLT of £1,304,124, that is 73.9% loan to cost, so check which of your lender's two caps binds first (LTGDV vs LTC).

Every total above is computed by the same engine as the appraisal workbench from the inputs listed. Change any input there to rerun the scheme on your own figures, and use the regional BCIS figures for the build cost.

SDLT on mixed-use land

A purchase that includes non-residential property is charged at the non-residential SDLT rates, and the residential higher-rates surcharge does not apply. The ledger uses the workbench's flat 5 percent allowance; work out the exact charge on the SDLT page.
