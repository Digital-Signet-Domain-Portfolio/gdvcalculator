East of England GDV Calculator and BCIS Build Cost

East of England build cost 1,453 to 1,911 £/sqm (BCIS estate housing quartiles rebased to Castle Point, July 2025). New-build sales values £3,900 to £4,800 per sqm (Castle Point (south Essex)). Values are Castle Point's, in south Essex; Cambridge and Norwich differ.

BCIS build cost per sqm

  • Estate housing, lower quartile: 1,453 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, median: 1,696 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, upper quartile: 1,911 £/sqm

BCIS figures rebased to Castle Point, as reprinted in Castle Point Plan Viability Study, Final Report (Porter Planning Economics for Castle Point Borough Council, July 2025, Table 5.6 and workshop slide 20). BCIS rebased to Castle Point at 3Q 2024. 5-year sample. BCIS rates are gross internal floor area, include preliminaries and exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT. BCIS itself is subscription-only; this is the most recent public reprint we found for the region.

New-build sales values for GDV

  • Canvey Island, houses: £3,900/sqm (about £362/sqft)
  • Canvey Island, flats: £4,250/sqm (about £395/sqft)
  • Mainland West and Central, houses: £4,400/sqm (about £409/sqft)
  • Mainland West and Central, flats: £4,300/sqm (about £399/sqft)
  • Mainland East, houses and flats: £4,800/sqm (about £446/sqft)

Values adopted for Castle Point (south Essex) in the Castle Point Plan Viability Study, Final Report (Porter Planning Economics for Castle Point Borough Council, July 2025, Table 5.2, tested average residential sales value by value area), based on Land Registry transactions January 2020 to July 2024 matched to EPC floor areas and indexed to July 2024 (5,240 sales, 145 new build). They are that study's figures for its own area, not a regional average. Studies publish £ per sqm; the per sqft figures are our conversion (divide by 10.7639). Check your own site against HM Land Registry Price Paid new-build sales before committing a bid. The workbench below loads: Castle Point, Mainland West and Central, houses.

Build cost and sales values are for Castle Point in south Essex.

The new-build evidence behind the values

  • Castle Point's study matched 5,240 Land Registry sales to EPC floor areas; 145 were new build (94 flats, 51 houses).
  • Its Table 5.1 shows new-build flats averaging £4,277 per sqm and new-build houses £4,374 per sqm, before the study set its tested values by area.

CIL across East councils

  • CIL is set council by council, and some councils charge none. Check the charging schedule and annual rate summary of the council where the site sits.
