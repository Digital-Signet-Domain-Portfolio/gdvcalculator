South West England GDV Calculator and BCIS Build Cost

South West England build cost 1,379 to 1,785 £/sqm (BCIS estate housing quartiles rebased to Cotswold, May 2026). New-build sales values £4,400 to £5,175 per sqm (Cotswold District). Values are Cotswold District's; Bristol and Cornwall differ.

BCIS build cost per sqm

  • Estate housing, lower quartile: 1,379 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, median: 1,559 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, upper quartile: 1,785 £/sqm
  • Flats, median: 1,832 £/sqm (quartiles 1,599 to 2,225)

BCIS figures rebased to Cotswold, as reprinted in Cotswold District Local Plan Viability Assessment (Quintic Advisory for Cotswold District Council, May 2026, Appendix 4). BCIS data last updated 16 May 2026, rebased to Cotswold (location index 101). 15-year default sample. BCIS rates are gross internal floor area, include preliminaries and exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT. BCIS itself is subscription-only; this is the most recent public reprint we found for the region.

New-build sales values for GDV

  • Fairford and Lechlade (lowest on the study's map): £4,400/sqm (about £409/sqft)
  • Cirencester: £4,665/sqm (about £433/sqft)
  • Middle of the nine values the study tests: £4,788/sqm (about £445/sqft)
  • Stow-on-the-Wold (highest on the study's map): £5,175/sqm (about £481/sqft)

Values adopted for Cotswold District in the Cotswold District Local Plan Viability Assessment (Quintic Advisory for Cotswold District Council, May 2026, Figure 2.18.1 and paragraph 4.2), based on 4,177 Land Registry transactions, March 2022 to March 2026, brought up to date with the House Price Index. They are that study's figures for its own area, not a regional average. Studies publish £ per sqm; the per sqft figures are our conversion (divide by 10.7639). Check your own site against HM Land Registry Price Paid new-build sales before committing a bid. The workbench below loads: Cotswold, middle of the nine tested values.

Build cost and sales values are for Cotswold District.

Small-sites threshold

  • The NPPF treats 10 or more homes as major development, which brings affordable housing and other policy asks.
  • BNG is a separate area test from the local-plan dwelling threshold: sites up to 0.2ha with no on-site priority habitat are exempt from 6 Aug 2026.
