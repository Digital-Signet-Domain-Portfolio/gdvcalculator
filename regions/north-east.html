North East GDV Calculator and BCIS Build Cost

North East build cost 1,188 to 1,533 £/sqm (BCIS estate housing quartiles rebased to Tees Valley, December 2024 (published 2025)). New-build sales values £2,200 to £3,300 per sqm (Middlesbrough). Values are Middlesbrough's; Newcastle and Sunderland differ.

BCIS build cost per sqm

  • Estate housing, lower quartile: 1,188 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, median: 1,345 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, upper quartile: 1,533 £/sqm
  • Flats, median: 1,554 £/sqm (quartiles 1,368 to 1,868)

BCIS figures rebased to Tees Valley, as reprinted in Middlesbrough Whole Plan Viability Assessment (HDH Planning and Development for Middlesbrough Council, December 2024 (published 2025), Appendix 11). BCIS data last updated 16 Nov 2024, rebased to Tees Valley (location index 91). 15-year default sample. BCIS rates are gross internal floor area, include preliminaries and exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT. BCIS itself is subscription-only; this is the most recent public reprint we found for the region.

New-build sales values for GDV

  • Lower value area (north), houses and flats: £2,200/sqm (about £204/sqft)
  • Medium value area, houses: £2,250/sqm (about £209/sqft)
  • Medium and higher value areas, flats: £2,300/sqm (about £214/sqft)
  • Higher value area (south), houses: £3,000/sqm (about £279/sqft)
  • Higher value area (south), bungalows: £3,300/sqm (about £307/sqft)

Values adopted for Middlesbrough in the Middlesbrough Whole Plan Viability Assessment (HDH Planning and Development for Middlesbrough Council, December 2024 (published 2025), Table 4.9, November 2024 post-consultation price assumptions), based on Land Registry prices paid (July 2023 and July 2024), Landmark data and a July 2024 survey of new-build asking prices. They are that study's figures for its own area, not a regional average. Studies publish £ per sqm; the per sqft figures are our conversion (divide by 10.7639). Check your own site against HM Land Registry Price Paid new-build sales before committing a bid. The workbench below loads: Middlesbrough medium value area, houses.

Build cost is rebased to Tees Valley and the sales values are for Middlesbrough.

How Middlesbrough set its values

  • The study's July 2024 survey found new-build asking prices from £149,995 to £800,000, averaging about £248,000 (£2,587 per sqm).
  • After consultation it cut the higher value area house figure from £3,300 to £3,000 per sqm (Table 4.7 against Table 4.9).

Viability test

  • PPG-Viability suggests 15 to 20 percent of GDV as a suitable developer return when plan policies are tested (paragraph 019); a scheme appraisal sets its own target, which the workbench lets you change.
