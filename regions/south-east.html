South East England GDV Calculator and BCIS Build Cost

South East England build cost 1,439 to 1,860 £/sqm (BCIS estate housing quartiles rebased to Medway, June 2025). GDV 420 to 680 £/sqft. Surrey, Kent and Sussex commuter belt pulls the mid band high; coastal Sussex and Hampshire anchor the floor.

BCIS build cost per sqm

  • Estate housing, lower quartile: 1,439 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, median: 1,628 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, upper quartile: 1,860 £/sqm
  • Flats, median: 1,886 £/sqm (quartiles 1,658 to 2,284)

BCIS figures rebased to Medway, as reprinted in Local Plan Viability Assessment 2025 Update (HDH Planning for Medway Council, June 2025, Appendix 10). BCIS data last updated 31 May 2025, rebased to Kent (location index 109). 15-year default sample. BCIS rates are gross internal floor area, include preliminaries and exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT. BCIS itself is subscription-only; this is the most recent public reprint we found for the region.

GDV working band

Resale GDV 420 to 680 £/sqft (low to high). Mid 525 £/sqft. Sense-check against current Land Registry Price Paid postcode-area aggregates before committing a bid.

Surrey, Kent, Sussex commuter belt premium versus coastal Sussex/Hampshire.

Viability culture

  • Most SE councils have adopted CIL and reference the 17.5 percent PoGDV PPG-Viability benchmark.
  • Sevenoaks, Guildford, Tunbridge Wells and Wokingham operate strict small-sites thresholds.

Working GDV anchors

  • Surrey commuter belt: £520 to £680/sqft.
  • Coastal Sussex / Hants: £420 to £525/sqft.
  • Always sense-check with Land Registry Price Paid before bidding.
