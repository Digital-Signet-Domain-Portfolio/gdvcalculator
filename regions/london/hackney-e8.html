Hackney E8 GDV Calculator

Hackney E8: borough CIL 293.44 £/sqm, Mayoral CIL2 zone Z2. The workbench prefills with the BCIS estate housing median rebased to Wandsworth and the lower end of the new-build sales values Wandsworth's viability study adopts (748 £/sqft); replace it with Hackney comparables.

CIL and GDV inputs

  • Borough CIL (residential): 293.44 £/sqm
  • Mayoral CIL2: Z2
  • New-build sales values adopted for Wandsworth site allocations: £8,050 to £11,400/sqm (about 748 to 1059 £/sqft), a Wandsworth figure rather than one for Hackney

Hackney's charging schedule (in effect since 1 April 2015) sets residential CIL at £190/sqm in Zone A, £25 in Zone B, £55 in Zone C and nil in Zone D. Dalston, Haggerston and London Fields in E8 sit in Zone A on the council's charging-zone map; the eastern edge towards Homerton is Zone B. Hackney publishes no annual rate summary, so the figure shown is our indexation of the £190 base: £190 x 400 / 259 (the RICS CIL index for 2026 over the index for 2015) = £293.44/sqm. Hackney's own CIL calculator gives the binding figure. Mayoral CIL2 adds £72.73/sqm (Zone 2, 2026-indexed, £60 base).

Local context

  • E8 (Dalston, London Fields). 35 percent affordable threshold above 10 units.
  • Class MA office-to-resi schemes pencil where commercial GDV gap is wide enough.
  • Hackney has historically scrutinised viability submissions; expect challenge on 17.5 percent PoGDV.
  • Hackney CIL Zone A £190/sqm base (£293.44 indexed to 2026) plus Mayoral CIL2 Zone 2 £72.73/sqm (2026-indexed, £60 base).
