Tower Hamlets E14 GDV Calculator

Tower Hamlets E14 GDV 700 to 1150 £/sqft. Borough CIL 335.33 £/sqm. Mayoral CIL2 zone Z2. Workbench prefills with the BCIS estate housing median rebased to Wandsworth and the borough mid-band GDV.

GDV and CIL working numbers

  • GDV: 700 to 1150 £/sqft
  • Mid GDV: 870 £/sqft
  • Borough CIL (residential): 335.33 £/sqm
  • Mayoral CIL2: Z2

Tower Hamlets' Annual CIL Rate Summary 2026 sets residential CIL at £335.33/sqm in Zone 1, £179.64 in Zone 2 and £59.88 in Zone 3 (2020 base rates £280, £150 and £50, indexed 400/334). E14 straddles zones: Canary Wharf and the northern Isle of Dogs are Zone 1, while the southern Isle of Dogs is Zone 2, on the residential charging-zone map in Appendix 1 of the council's CIL Charging Schedule 2020. Poplar is split between zones, so check that map for the exact site. The figure shown is the Zone 1 rate. Mayoral CIL2 adds £72.73/sqm (Zone 2, 2026-indexed, £60 base).

Local context

  • E14 covers Canary Wharf, Limehouse and the Isle of Dogs.
  • Tower Hamlets CIL2 for 2026: Zone 1 £335.33/sqm, Zone 2 £179.64/sqm, Zone 3 £59.88/sqm (Annual CIL Rate Summary 2026). On the council's 2020 charging-zone map, Canary Wharf and the northern Isle of Dogs are Zone 1 and the southern Isle of Dogs is Zone 2; Poplar is split between zones, so check the map for the exact site.
  • Mayoral CIL2 Zone 2 £72.73/sqm (2026-indexed, £60 base) stacks on borough rate.
