North West GDV Calculator and BCIS Build Cost

North West build cost 1,466 to 1,960 £/sqm (BCIS estate housing quartiles rebased to Oldham, December 2025). New-build sales values £1,774 to £4,400 per sqm (Oldham). Figures are rebased to Oldham, which the BCIS printout gives a location index of 101. Sales values are Oldham's; Manchester and Liverpool differ.

BCIS build cost per sqm

  • Estate housing, lower quartile: 1,466 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, median: 1,745 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, upper quartile: 1,960 £/sqm
  • Flats, median: 1,997 £/sqm (quartiles 1,638 to 2,435)

BCIS figures rebased to Oldham, as reprinted in Oldham Local Plan Viability Assessment (publication plan) (Aspinall Verdi for Oldham Council, December 2025, Appendix 8). BCIS data last updated 4 Oct 2025, rebased to Oldham (location index 101). 5-year sample. BCIS rates are gross internal floor area, include preliminaries and exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT. BCIS itself is subscription-only; this is the most recent public reprint we found for the region.

New-build sales values for GDV

  • 2 bed apartment, lower zone: £1,774/sqm (about £165/sqft)
  • 2 bed apartment, medium zone: £2,097/sqm (about £195/sqft)
  • 2 bed apartment, higher zone: £2,419/sqm (about £225/sqft)
  • 2 bed house, higher zone: £4,384/sqm (about £407/sqft)
  • 3 bed house, lower zone: £2,451/sqm (about £228/sqft)
  • 3 bed house, medium zone: £3,284/sqm (about £305/sqft)
  • 4+ bed house, higher zone: £4,400/sqm (about £409/sqft)

Values adopted for Oldham in the Oldham Local Plan Viability Assessment (publication plan) (Aspinall Verdi for Oldham Council, December 2025, Table 6.10, £ psm value assumptions (lower, medium and higher value zones)), based on Land Registry new-build house sales 2023 to 2025, second-hand values, asking prices and stakeholder consultation (value assumptions dated September 2025). They are that study's figures for its own area, not a regional average. Studies publish £ per sqm; the per sqft figures are our conversion (divide by 10.7639). Check your own site against HM Land Registry Price Paid new-build sales before committing a bid. The workbench below loads: Oldham 2 bed house, higher value zone.

Build cost and sales values are for Oldham.

BCIS location index

  • BCIS sets its location factors against a UK mean of 100 (BCIS basics: using location factors in construction, 29 July 2025). The Oldham printout in the council's viability assessment reads “Rebased to Oldham (101; sample 30)”, so Oldham sits just above the UK mean.
  • BCIS publishes its full location factor table only to subscribers; a figure rebased to a different town will differ.

Oldham value zones

  • Oldham's study maps three value zones by ward (Figure 6.8): Saddleworth sits in the higher value zone and central Oldham in the lower.
  • See the 9-unit Manchester M4 worked scenario for a full ledger; its GDV is that scenario's own stated assumption.
