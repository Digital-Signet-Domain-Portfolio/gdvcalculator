North West GDV Calculator and BCIS Build Cost

North West build cost 1,466 to 1,960 £/sqm (BCIS estate housing quartiles rebased to Oldham, December 2025). GDV 235 to 425 £/sqft. Figures are rebased to Oldham, which the BCIS printout gives a location index of 101.

BCIS build cost per sqm

  • Estate housing, lower quartile: 1,466 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, median: 1,745 £/sqm
  • Estate housing, upper quartile: 1,960 £/sqm
  • Flats, median: 1,997 £/sqm (quartiles 1,638 to 2,435)

BCIS figures rebased to Oldham, as reprinted in Oldham Local Plan Viability Assessment (publication plan) (Aspinall Verdi for Oldham Council, December 2025, Appendix 8). BCIS data last updated 4 Oct 2025, rebased to Oldham (location index 101). 5-year sample. BCIS rates are gross internal floor area, include preliminaries and exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT. BCIS itself is subscription-only; this is the most recent public reprint we found for the region.

GDV working band

Resale GDV 235 to 425 £/sqft (low to high). Mid 305 £/sqft. Sense-check against current Land Registry Price Paid postcode-area aggregates before committing a bid.

Manchester, Liverpool, Chester.

BCIS location index

  • BCIS sets its location factors against a UK mean of 100 (BCIS basics: using location factors in construction, 29 July 2025). The Oldham printout in the council's viability assessment reads “Rebased to Oldham (101; sample 30)”, so Oldham sits just above the UK mean.
  • BCIS publishes its full location factor table only to subscribers; a figure rebased to a different town will differ.
