North West (BCIS baseline) BCIS Q2-2026: 1,950 to 2,750 £/sqm. GDV 235 to 425 £/sqft. Manchester, Liverpool and Chester anchor the upper band. BCIS regional baseline (index 100).

BCIS Q2 2026 band

  • Low: 1,950 £/sqm
  • Mid: 2,300 £/sqm
  • High: 2,750 £/sqm
  • Regional index (UK = 100): 100

GDV working band

Resale GDV 235 to 425 £/sqft (low to high). Mid 305 £/sqft. Sense-check against current Land Registry Price Paid postcode-area aggregates before committing a bid.

BCIS regional baseline (index 100). Manchester, Liverpool, Chester.

Why NW is the baseline

  • BCIS publishes regional adjustment factors against a UK-average index of 100, with NW historically used as the working baseline because of large-sample tender-price coverage.
  • Read other regions' rates as a relative adjustment from this base.

M4 scheme economics

  • Ancoats / M4 small-sites schemes deliver 9 to 12 unit blocks at 260k GDV per unit.
  • See the 9-unit M4 worked scenario for the full ledger.
