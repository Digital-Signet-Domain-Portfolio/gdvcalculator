London (Outer) GDV Calculator (BCIS Q2 2026)

London (Outer) BCIS Q2-2026: 2,650 to 3,700 £/sqm. GDV 620 to 1200 £/sqft. Inner London adds 5 to 12 percent on build cost and lifts GDV materially. CIL Mayoral CIL2 stacks on top of borough CIL.

BCIS Q2 2026 band

  • Low: 2,650 £/sqm
  • Mid: 3,050 £/sqm
  • High: 3,700 £/sqm
  • Regional index (UK = 100): 119

GDV working band

Resale GDV 620 to 1200 £/sqft (low to high). Mid 780 £/sqft. Sense-check against current Land Registry Price Paid postcode-area aggregates before committing a bid.

Outer London BCIS; Inner London adds a further 5 to 12 percent on prime sites.

Mayoral CIL2 zones

  • Zone 1: £96.97/sqm for permissions granted in 2026 (£80 base). Camden, City of London, Westminster, Hammersmith & Fulham, Islington, Kensington & Chelsea, Richmond, Wandsworth.
  • Zone 2: £72.73/sqm (£60 base). Hackney, Lewisham, Southwark, Tower Hamlets, Lambeth, Ealing, Brent and most other inner-outer boroughs.
  • Zone 3: £30.30/sqm (£25 base). Barking & Dagenham, Bexley, Croydon, Greenwich, Havering, Newham, Sutton.
  • Rates index annually by the RICS CIL Index (Ip 400 / Ic 330 in 2026). Borough CIL stacks on top; always check the borough's adopted charging schedule.

Which London boroughs publish viability TPDPL

  • Camden, Hackney, Lewisham, Southwark, Tower Hamlets and Wandsworth publish tariff schedules and small-sites thresholds.
  • See the borough deep-dives for working numbers.
