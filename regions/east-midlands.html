East Midlands GDV Calculator (BCIS Q2 2026)

East Midlands BCIS Q2-2026: 2,050 to 2,850 £/sqm. GDV 250 to 400 £/sqft. Nottingham, Derby and Leicester. Sub-250k GDV per unit pencils on small sites under the right cost stack.

BCIS Q2 2026 band

  • Low: 2,050 £/sqm
  • Mid: 2,400 £/sqm
  • High: 2,850 £/sqm
  • Regional index (UK = 100): 95

GDV working band

Resale GDV 250 to 400 £/sqft (low to high). Mid 310 £/sqft. Sense-check against current Land Registry Price Paid postcode-area aggregates before committing a bid.

Nottingham, Derby, Leicester; sub-250k GDV/unit pencils on small sites.

Viability test

  • NPPF Dec 2024 17.5 percent PoGDV benchmark is the working test.
  • Schemes that target sub-250k GDV per unit must control build cost to under £1,800/sqm to hit the benchmark.

Sub-250k GDV per unit

  • Terrace stock outside city centres delivers 220k to 280k GDV per unit.
  • Below 250k, professional fees must trim to 9 to 10 percent or PoC dips below 17.5 percent.
