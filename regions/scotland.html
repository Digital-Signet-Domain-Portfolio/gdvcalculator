GDV Calculator/Regions/Scotland

Scotland GDV Calculator and BCIS Build Cost

Scotland build cost 1,295 to 1,421 £/sqm (Valuation Office Agency for the Cairngorms National Park Authority, 2 October 2024). New-build sales values £2,813 to £3,382 per sqm (Cairngorms National Park (six allocated sites)). LBTT plus ADS apply to land purchase. Values are for Cairngorms National Park sites; Edinburgh and Glasgow differ.

BCIS build cost per sqm

  • Adopted range: 1,295 to 1,421 £/sqm

Valuation Office Agency for the Cairngorms National Park Authority, Market Value Report, affordable housing viability (2 October 2024): Average build costs adopted between the BCIS lower quartile and mean for the local authorities containing each site. We found no public document reprinting BCIS quartiles for Scotland; this range covers rural national park sites and publishes no median.

New-build sales values for GDV

  • Lowest: Newtonmore, 3 bed terraced: £2,813/sqm (about £261/sqft)
  • Newtonmore, all private units: £2,947/sqm (about £274/sqft)
  • North Aviemore, all private units: £3,037/sqm (about £282/sqft)
  • Highest: Blair Atholl, 2 bed terraced: £3,382/sqm (about £314/sqft)

Values adopted for Cairngorms National Park (six allocated sites) in the Market Value Report, affordable housing viability (Valuation Office Agency for the Cairngorms National Park Authority, 2 October 2024, Appendices 7.1 to 7.6, private sales GDV per site), based on local and nearby sales, schemes then being marketed and weighted second-hand sales (sites inspected around July 2024). They are that study's figures for its own area, not a regional average. Studies publish £ per sqm; the per sqft figures are our conversion (divide by 10.7639). Check your own site against HM Land Registry Price Paid new-build sales before committing a bid. The workbench below loads: North Aviemore, all private units.

Build cost and sales values are for Cairngorms National Park sites. LBTT and ADS apply to the land buy.

LBTT and ADS

  • LBTT (Revenue Scotland) replaces SDLT. ADS adds 8 percent on residential buys above £40,000 for companies and second-property buyers.

NPF4

  • National Planning Framework 4 (Scottish Government) is Scotland's national planning policy.
  • It differs from the NPPF, including an infrastructure-first approach and 20-minute neighbourhoods.
