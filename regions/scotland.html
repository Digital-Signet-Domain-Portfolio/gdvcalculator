GDV Calculator/Regions/Scotland

Scotland GDV Calculator and BCIS Build Cost

Scotland build cost 1,295 to 1,421 £/sqm (Valuation Office Agency for the Cairngorms National Park Authority, 2 October 2024). GDV 250 to 470 £/sqft. LBTT plus ADS apply to land purchase; NPF4 governs viability.

BCIS build cost per sqm

  • Adopted range: 1,295 to 1,421 £/sqm

Valuation Office Agency for the Cairngorms National Park Authority, Market Value Report, affordable housing viability (2 October 2024): Average build costs adopted between the BCIS lower quartile and mean for the local authorities containing each site. We found no public document reprinting BCIS quartiles for Scotland; this range covers rural national park sites and publishes no median.

GDV working band

Resale GDV 250 to 470 £/sqft (low to high). Mid 320 £/sqft. Sense-check against current Land Registry Price Paid postcode-area aggregates before committing a bid.

LBTT and ADS apply; NPF4 viability tests differ from NPPF.

LBTT and ADS

  • LBTT (Revenue Scotland) replaces SDLT. ADS adds 8 percent on residential buys above £40,000 for companies and second-property buyers.
  • Edinburgh anchors the upper band; Glasgow and Aberdeen sit lower.

NPF4

  • National Planning Framework 4 (Scottish Government) sets the viability test.
  • Differs from NPPF in emphasis on infrastructure-first delivery and 20-minute neighbourhoods.
