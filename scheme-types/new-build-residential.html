What this preset is for

Medium-rise blocks or terraces. CIL and BNG itemised per unit.

What BCIS rate to use

  • BCIS is subscription data, so this site uses BCIS figures as reprinted in council local plan viability studies, one per region. The preset loads the North West flats median; houses use the estate housing figures.
  • Regional lower quartile, median and upper quartile figures, with the study, date and rebasing for each, are on the regional BCIS table at /regions.
  • BCIS figures include preliminaries but exclude external works, fees, contingency and VAT. The workbench adds fees and contingency; add an allowance for external works to the build cost yourself.

Viability under NPPF Aug 2026

  • PPG-Viability suggests a 15 to 20 percent profit-on-GDV range; 17.5 percent (the midpoint) is the working benchmark.
  • Lenders typically reference 20 percent profit-on-cost; the two are not interchangeable.
