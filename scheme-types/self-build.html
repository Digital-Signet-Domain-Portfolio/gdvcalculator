Self-build, single dwelling

Single dwelling self-build with HMRC VAT reclaim on materials. Target return lower than commercial development.

Preset defaults

  • Units: 1
  • GIA per unit: 2,000 sqft
  • NIA ratio: 95%
  • Build cost: 2,200 £/sqm
  • Professional fees: 8%
  • Contingency: 10%
  • s106 / CIL / BNG per unit: £0
  • Finance rate: 9.75%
  • Duration: 14 months
  • Target PoC: 15%

What this preset is for

Single dwelling self-build with HMRC VAT reclaim on materials. Target return lower than commercial development.

Build cost by spec

  • Basic: £1,800 to £2,000/sqm.
  • Mid: £2,100 to £2,400/sqm.
  • High: 2,500 to 3,500+ £/sqm (passivhaus, prime finishes).

VAT reclaim

  • HMRC VAT431NB DIY housebuilders reclaim recovers 20 percent VAT on materials.
  • Labour from a VAT-registered builder is zero-rated; do not reclaim.
  • Digital portal active; claim within 6 months of completion.
