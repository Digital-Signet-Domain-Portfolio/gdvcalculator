GDV Calculator/About

About GDV Calculator

GDV Calculator is a free residual appraisal workbench for UK property developers. Data integrity and product by Oliver Wakefield-Smith, Founder of Digital Signet. Model reviewed by a chartered surveyor (MRICS) and a chartered tax adviser (CTA). No affiliate links on the calculator output.

Who built it

Oliver Wakefield-Smith, Founder of Digital Signet. Background in data engineering, financial analysis and property investment. Owns the data layer (BCIS regional bands, sources of record, calculator engine).

Who reviews the model

A chartered surveyor (MRICS) reviews residual method, BCIS rate bands, viability framing. A chartered tax adviser (CTA / ATT) reviews SDLT, LBTT, LTT and VAT mechanics.

Conflicts of interest

There are no affiliate links on the calculator output. The /compare page lists competitor tools without referral fees. The site does not receive commission on bridging, dev finance or any other product. If that changes you will see it disclosed on the affected page.

Governance and updates

BCIS rates refreshed on quarterly cuts. NPPF / PPG-Viability monitored for revisions. SDLT / LBTT / LTT rate bands re-verified each Budget. Changelog records the audit trail.

Corrections

If you spot a figure that is wrong, email oliver@digitalsignet.com with the page URL and the source citation. Corrections are typically deployed within 48 hours.
