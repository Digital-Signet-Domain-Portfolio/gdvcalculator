Senior vs Mezzanine Development Finance

Senior development debt is a first charge capped at 65 to 70 percent LTGDV by the lenders that publish their terms. West One prices it from 5.00 percent plus Bank Rate and Maslow Capital quotes margins from 3.8 percent; Shawbrook says its rates are negotiable. Mezzanine sits behind senior under an inter-creditor deed with second-charge security. None of the lenders we checked publishes a mezzanine rate. Pref-equity replaces mezzanine in some structures and ranks behind both.

Stack mechanics

  • Senior: first charge, up to 65 to 70 percent LTGDV and 80 to 90 percent LTC depending on the lender.
  • Stretch senior: one lender lends deeper instead of adding a mezzanine lender. Hodge Bank publishes stretch senior up to 75 percent LTGDV and 88 percent LTC.
  • Mezzanine: West One, which lends senior only, describes mezzanine lenders as typically funding up to 75 percent LTGDV and 90 percent LTC.
  • Equity covers the remainder.

Inter-creditor terms

The inter-creditor deed sets the payment waterfall, standstill rights and step-in. Mezzanine usually takes a second charge over the SPV's land and a pledge over the SPV shares. Senior approves any change to mezzanine terms.

Pref-equity alternative

Pref-equity ranks behind both senior and mezzanine and takes equity, not a charge, with a preferred return agreed deal by deal. It is used where mezzanine is unavailable or where the sponsor wants to keep the debt off its own balance sheet.

    Max 65% LTGDV and 85% LTC; interest from 5.00% + Bank Rate

  • Hampshire Trust Bank
    Up to 65% of GDV for experienced developers
    development finance page
  • United Trust Bank
    Up to 65% LTGDV and 90% LTC (Sept 2025 product guide); its web page says up to 70% LTGDV
    product guide PG.0925 and web page
  • Aldermore
    Up to 70% LTGDV / 85% LTC; bespoke pricing
    property development finance page
  • Maslow Capital
    Up to 70% of GDV and 90% of cost, for larger schemes; margins from 3.8%
    development finance page

Each lender's own website or product guide, read 24 November 2026. Terms change often and depend on the scheme and the borrower; a lender's maximum is not an offer.
